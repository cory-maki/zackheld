About Dr. Held

Building Psychologically Healthy Institutions

Dr. Zachary David Held is a doctoral-level psychologist whose career has centered on strengthening the systems that support students, trainees, and professionals in higher education and healthcare. His work addresses the intersection of organizational culture, graduate preparation, and evidence-driven well-being policy.

With postdoctoral training in pediatric oncology, hematology, and BMT psychology, Dr. Held developed deep expertise in high-stakes communication, trauma-sensitive practice, and interdisciplinary collaboration — insights he applies at the systems level to academic and medical training environments. Learn more about his approach to behavioral health program development and institutional leadership.

As an instructor and mentor across multiple graduate psychology programs, Zack Held PhD has shaped the preparation of emerging practitioners, with a consistent emphasis on clinical competence, ethical practice, and professional sustainability. His work on student well-being and academic persistence reflects this long-standing commitment to trainee success.
